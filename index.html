How to read the data

Recreation.govUsually 6 months ahead. A new date opens each day at 7:00 AM Pacific (10:00 AM Eastern). Some campgrounds use monthly blocks, shorter windows or lotteries.
ReserveCalifornia6 months to the day, with a new date each day at 8:00 AM Pacific.
Month-end datesIf the date 6 months earlier does not exist (for example Aug 31), the last day of that month is used.
VerifiedAn official page or several agreeing sources.
Needs checkingA secondary or conflicting source. The fields to confirm are listed on each campground.
Not yet researchedThe booking site's standard release rule is applied. Season, peak season and walk-up are unknown.
