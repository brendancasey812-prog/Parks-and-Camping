About this data

Unofficial. Not affiliated with Recreation.gov, ReserveCalifornia, the National Park Service or California State Parks. Always confirm on the booking site before you rely on a date.

  • Recreation.gov usually opens each new date 6 months ahead at 7:00 AM Pacific (10:00 AM Eastern). Some campgrounds use monthly blocks (Yosemite's Pines, Wawona and Hodgdon Meadow open one month at a time on the 15th), shorter windows or lotteries.
  • ReserveCalifornia opens each new date 6 months to the day at 8:00 AM Pacific.
  • "Date available" is computed in Pacific time from your arrival date and shown in the time zone you pick. If the 6-month date does not exist (for example Aug 31), the last day of that month is used.
  • Data check: Confirmed official page or several agreeing sources. Needs checking a secondary source or conflicting sources; the fields to verify are listed in each row. Built from a web search on ; the daily check (see docs/DAILY-SEARCH.md) will confirm and refresh it.
