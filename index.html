        About

        Unofficial personal tracker. This is not an official site of, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the National Park Service or California State Parks. Your checks and edits are saved in this browser only (see Backup above).

        Unit names, designations, states and park codes come from the NPS_Park_Units spreadsheet (475 units; Sequoia and Kings Canyon are shown as two parks). Map coordinates are approximate, and units marked with a dashed dot are placed near their state center. Verify against nps.gov/findapark.

        Basemap: shaded relief rendered from AWS Terrain Tiles (NASA SRTM, USGS 3DEP, GEBCO), with Natural Earth and US Census boundaries. It ships with the site, so the map loads without any outside map service.
