# Data audit, 2026-11-02

Every campground on the site was checked against the official sources that this environment can reach:
nps.gov campground pages, Recreation.gov's public campground data (`/api/camps/campgrounds/<id>` and
`/api/search/campsites`), and each state park's page on parks.ca.gov. ReserveCalifornia's site loads, but its
campground data comes from a separate service that was not used. Changed values carry their official source and
`"checked": "2026-11-02"` in `data/seasons.js`.

## Corrections made

- Crane Flat, White Wolf, Yosemite Creek, Porcupine Flat, Bridalveil Creek, Tamarack Flat: **2 weeks ahead** (was 6 months).
- Sequoia & Kings Canyon (Recreation.gov windows): Lodgepole, Potwisha, Azalea, Canyon View, Sentinel **4 months**;
  Dorst Creek, Cold Springs, Buckeye Flat, Atwell Mill, Sunset, Sheep Creek, Moraine **1 month**. Release time assumed 7:00 AM Pacific.

**Open dates and closures**
- Yosemite 2026 dates from the NPS table (North Pines to Oct 26, Camp 4 reservations Apr 15 – Nov 29, Crane Flat May 21 – Oct 12,
  White Wolf Jul 2 – Sep 14, Bridalveil Creek Jul 3 – Aug 14, Tuolumne Meadows Jul 1 – Sep 27, ...).
- Seacliff State Beach: **closed** since the January 2023 storms (was shown open year-round).
- D.L. Bliss: camping closed until May 2027. Emerald Bay (Eagle Point): closed for the 2026 season.
- Point Mugu, Thornhill Broome: closed from Oct 8, 2026 (high tide). Salton Sea, Mecca Beach: closed for repairs.
- Pfeiffer Big Sur: reopened Oct 16, 2026 after the Timber Fire. Austin Creek (Bullfrog Pond): closed.
- South Fork (Sequoia): closed indefinitely. Crags (Lassen): not listed by NPS in 2026.

## Still to verify
- Death Valley's first-come campgrounds, Point Reyes' four camps, Devils Postpile and Rob Hill: no individual official listing found.
- Crystal Springs (Sequoia): Recreation.gov shows a 2-day window, which looks like a data error; not changed.
- Leo Carrillo: the park page lists a "Superintendent Order: Campground Closure" without dates; not changed.
- Clear Lake has four campgrounds (Cole Creek 26 sites, Kelsey Creek, Lower and Upper Bayview); total not confirmed.
- Hungry Valley, Oceano Dunes and Ocotillo Wells: parks.ca.gov pages were blocked (ohv.parks.ca.gov is not in the allowed list).
- Peak seasons are not published by these sources; they remain the web-search / California State Parks peak-rate values.
